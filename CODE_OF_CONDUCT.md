# Code of conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone.

Be respectful, constructive, and considerate. Do not publish another person's private information, recordings, transcripts, credentials, or identifying metadata without explicit permission. Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when necessary to protect the community. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's security contact. Maintainers will review reports promptly, protect reporter privacy, and respond proportionately.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project elsewhere.
